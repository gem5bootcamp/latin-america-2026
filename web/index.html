About the bootcamp

From your first configuration script to research-ready simulations.

This workshop introduces processor simulation through gem5, then builds toward the skills needed to explore architecture research questions with confidence.

No prior gem5 experience is assumed. Across the week, participants will work with processors, caches, memory systems, traffic generation, simulation statistics, SimObjects, checkpoints, fast-forwarding, and advanced simulation workflows.

01

Learn by doing

Hands-on sessions and examples keep the focus on building, running, and modifying real gem5 models.

02

Build research intuition

Connect simulation mechanisms to the kinds of architectural questions researchers investigate with gem5.

03

Keep experimenting

Use homework and office hours to turn the week's tutorials into your own experimental ideas.
